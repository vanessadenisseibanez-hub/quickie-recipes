🍜 Pancit Bihon Recipe

Pancit Bihon

Preparation Time: 35 minutes (approx.)

Ingredients

  • 250g bihon (rice vermicelli noodles)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup sliced chicken or shrimp
  • 1 cup sliced vegetables (carrots, cabbage, snow peas)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Calamansi or lemon wedges for serving

Instructions

  1. Soak the bihon noodles in hot water until softened (about 5-10 minutes), then drain.
  2. Sauté garlic and onion in oil until fragrant. Add chicken/shrimp and cook until done.
  3. Add vegetables and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  4. Pour in soy sauce and chicken broth. Bring to a simmer.
  5. Add the drained bihon noodles. Toss constantly until the noodles have absorbed most of the liquid.
  6. Season with salt and pepper. Serve hot, garnished with a squeeze of calamansi.
