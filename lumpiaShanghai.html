Lumpiang Shanghai (Filipino Spring Rolls)

Preparation Time: 30 minutes (approx.)

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1 carrot, finely minced
  • 1 onion, finely minced
  • 1/4 cup spring onion, chopped
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 30-40 lumpia wrappers
  • Cooking oil for deep frying

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, mix ground pork, minced vegetables, egg, soy sauce, salt, and pepper until well combined.
  2. Lay a wrapper flat. Place about 1-2 teaspoons of the mixture near one edge.
  3. Fold the sides inward, then roll tightly away from you to form a log. Seal the edge with a little water or egg wash.
  4. Heat oil in a deep pot to medium-high heat.
  5. Fry the lumpia in batches until golden brown and crispy. Drain excess oil on paper towels.
  6. Serve with a sweet and sour dipping sauce.
