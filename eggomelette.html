🍳 Egg Omelette Recipe

Egg Omelette

Preparation Time: 5 minutes (approx.)

Ingredients

  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp milk (optional, for fluffiness)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1/2 tbsp butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: chopped onions, tomatoes, cheese

Instructions

  1. Whisk the eggs, milk (if using), salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  2. Heat butter or oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour the egg mixture into the pan.
  4. If using fillings, sprinkle them over the egg immediately.
  5. Cook until the edges are set. Gently fold the omelette in half.
  6. Cook for another minute until done. Serve immediately.
