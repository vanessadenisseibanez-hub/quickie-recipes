🍆 Tortang Talong (Eggplant Omelette) Recipe

Tortang Talong (Eggplant Omelette)

Preparation Time: 20 minutes (approx.)

Ingredients

  • 2 medium eggplants (talong)
  • 3 large eggs, beaten
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (optional)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Cooking oil for frying

Instructions

  1. Grill or roast the eggplants until the skin is charred and the flesh is soft.
  2. Peel the skin off the eggplants, keeping the stem intact. Flatten the flesh with a fork.
  3. Season the beaten eggs with salt and pepper (and garlic).
  4. Dip the flattened eggplant into the beaten egg mixture.
  5. Fry in a pan with oil until the egg is cooked and golden brown on both sides.
  6. Serve with a dipping sauce of choice (e.g., soy sauce and vinegar).
