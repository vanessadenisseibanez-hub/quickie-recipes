🍗 Chicken Adobo Recipe

Chicken Adobo

Preparation Time: 45-60 minutes (approx.)

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces (e.g., drumsticks, thighs)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup white vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 1 tsp whole peppercorns
  • 2-3 bay leaves (laurel)
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil (optional)

Instructions

  1. In a pot, combine chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, crushed garlic, peppercorns, and bay leaves.
  2. Bring to a boil, then immediately lower the heat and simmer, uncovered, for 10-15 minutes. **Do not stir initially.**
  3. Cover and continue to simmer for 30-45 minutes or until the chicken is tender and cooked through, stirring occasionally.
  4. Remove the chicken from the pot. Increase heat and reduce the sauce to your desired consistency.
  5. Return the chicken to the sauce and coat. Serve hot with rice.
